Ask a question
What documents do I need to import goods into the DRC?

Usually a commercial invoice, packing list and bill of lading or air waybill, plus any certificates required for your type of goods. We check your file and tell you exactly what's needed before shipping.

How long does shipping take?

It depends on the origin, the transport mode and customs. Air freight is the fastest; sea freight is the most economical for large volumes. We give you an estimated transit time with every quote.

Can you collect goods from my supplier abroad?

Yes. We offer door-to-door service: pick-up from your supplier, transport, customs clearance and delivery to your address.

Is my cargo insured?

Cargo insurance is available on request. Tell us the value of your goods when asking for a quote and we'll include the insurance cost.

How do I get updates on my shipment?

Send your tracking, B/L or AWB number via the tracking box or WhatsApp, and our team will reply with the latest status.

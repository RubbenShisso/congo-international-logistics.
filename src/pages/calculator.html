Estimates only, using standard industry ratios (sea 1 m³ = 1,000 kg · air 6,000 cm³ = 1 kg). Your final price is confirmed in your quote.

CBM

Cubic metre: length × width × height in metres. Used to price sea freight.

Volumetric weight

Light but bulky cargo is charged by the space it takes, not only by its weight.

Chargeable weight

The higher of the real weight and the volumetric weight — this is what you pay for.

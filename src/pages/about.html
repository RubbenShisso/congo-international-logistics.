Who we are

Your gateway to Central Africa

Congo International Logistics SARL is a freight forwarding, logistics and trading company headquartered in Limete, Kinshasa, with a presence in South Africa. We connect businesses in the DRC with suppliers and markets around the world.

Our team knows the local ports, borders and regulations, so your goods move faster, safer and with full transparency.

  • Local expertise in DRC customs & borders
  • Competitive rates and honest pricing
  • Real-time updates via WhatsApp & email
  • Cargo insurance available
